The model

A → Agent → B

The agent isn't for the prospect, and it isn't a research tool for its own sake. It exists to move one thing: what's true about B's operational pain, into A's hands, before the call.

A — Sales rep
Not enough time, too many tabs

Account executives selling tax, compliance, and logistics software into mid-market and SME accounts, juggling 10–15 live prospects a week.

→
Agent — MAVERICK
Detects & translates pain

Runs targeted searches per prospect, grounds every signal in a real source, and converts it into a sourced, meeting-ready brief.

→
B — Prospect company
Real, identifiable pain

CFOs, finance directors, and logistics heads already fighting ITC mismatches, refund delays, and customs holds — the raw material for the brief.

Signal domains

Four places prospects are already hurting

Each domain below is grounded in documented, sourced pain signals — not assumptions about what a company "might" be dealing with.

GST & ITCSignals 1–3

Filing windows that punish being ready

Portal downtime during peak filing periods, core-field amendments stuck pending for weeks, and reconciliation workflows that force manual re-uploads — all while ITC interest penalties keep accruing at 18–24% annually.

Anchor: 39% of invoices carry errors requiring manual investigation before filing.
LogisticsSignals 4–7

Tracking blackouts, silent weight disputes

Shipment status freezes with no courier accountability, fraudulent RTO markings that push cost back onto the seller, and weight-dispute notifications that arrive weeks after the 7-day contest window has closed.

Anchor: 82% of logistics operations report manual documentation as a heavy-to-extreme drag on efficiency.
CustomsSignals 8–9

One glitch, every shipment at the port

ICEGATE payment and DSC-authentication failures freeze duty payments outright, cascading into RBI forex compliance flags — for errors the importer never caused and can't self-resolve.

Anchor: ~30% of shipment delays trace back to HS code misclassification.
Foreign Trade PolicySignal 10

Penalised for the portal's own bugs

DGFT incentive schemes have applied submission-date logic that silently docked exporters for system errors, with no audit trail to appeal — a pattern that erodes trust in digital filing itself.

Anchor: 648+ annual compliance obligations tracked, largely by hand, at a single-state logistics company.
